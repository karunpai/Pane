# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- **Display layouts.** After you plug in a monitor, double-tap a key within 1 minute to arrange your displays: **Option** twice puts the Mac on the left of the monitor, **Command** twice puts it below. Works with any monitor.
- **New Displays tab** in Settings: turn display arrangement on or off, edit the two layouts or add your own (position, alignment, menu bar, trigger key), preview them, and apply one instantly.
- `Pane --apply-layout "<name>"` applies a saved layout from the command line.
